--- layout: default body_class: is-home scripts: - /assets/js/stars.js - /assets/js/path.js --- {%- comment -%} The whole site on one page, set like a book. The prologue: the arched portrait in the rail, with the welcome line under it like an inscription; the introduction beside it; and under both, the facts (research interests, contact), labels in the rail and values beside them. Then every chapter in _chapters/ in `order`: its title in the rail, its epigraph and content beside it. A chapter with `matter: back` (Acknowledgments) is back matter, set quieter. A `wide` chapter (Notes, Life) sets its content under the head, across the full width. The stars (assets/js/stars.js) drift behind the whole page. They steer around the text marked data-stars-avoid, the text that sits on the bare background; cards and the portrait simply hide them as they pass behind. Elements marked data-reveal rise and fade in: the prologue's on load (after their --reveal-delay), a chapter's as they scroll up into the window, in step with the scroll.
